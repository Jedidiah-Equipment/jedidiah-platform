# Choose your alerts

The phone app's **Settings** page shows what the app is allowed to use on your phone and which alerts you get. It is the same page in Contracting and Equipment, and it is not the Contracting **Settings** page on the web.

1. Tap the **⋯** menu at the top right, then **Settings**.
2. Under **Permissions**, each thing the app uses shows its state:
   - **Allowed**: nothing to do.
   - **Not asked yet**: tap **Allow** and answer the phone's question.
   - **Not allowed**: tap **Allow** if the button shows; otherwise tap **Open Settings**, turn it on in the phone's settings, and come back to the app. The row updates by itself.
3. Under **Notifications**, each alert your role can get has a switch. Turn one off to stop that alert reaching you; turn it on again to get it back.

| Permission | What the app uses it for |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | Workshop alerts. Shown only if your role gets alerts. |
| Camera | Photos of meters, Breakdowns and Field Notes, and scanning labels. |
| Microphone | Voice Notes. |
| Location | Placing a Breakdown report. |
| Photos | Saving the photos you take to the Jedidiah album. iPhone only. |

Mobile web has no **Permissions**: the browser asks the first time something needs one.

## What turning an alert off does

- It stops that alert's push notifications to you, on every phone you sign in to.
- It does not change what you see in the app: the Workshop queue, **Activity** and the Dashboard are the same.
- It does not change anyone else's alerts.

If your role gets no alerts, **Notifications** says so and the app never asks for notification permission. [Who gets workshop alerts](./who-gets-workshop-alerts) lists which roles get which alert.
