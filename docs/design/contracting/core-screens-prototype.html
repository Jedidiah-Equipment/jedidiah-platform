Jedidiah Contracting Screens

The core screens per role, rendering the decisions on map #1359. Structure and flow are the question; visual polish is not. Every frame is annotated with the decision it draws from.

Foreman — the phone in the field

Sees only jobs assigned to him. Selects, never types (hours excepted); money never appears. Two home-screen variants below — pick one.

Henk — contracting manager

Creates and assigns jobs, watches the queues, signs off. The sign-off screen is where gaps get resolved and dates get stamped.

Connor — workshop manager

Owns every breakdown transition, assigns one primary mechanic, records services. Reads everything; writes workshop.

Jed — pricing, fleet, utilisation

Pricing and Preset Rates sit with contracting-admin and super-admin alone. Fleet and the utilisation view answer "what do we own and is it working?"

Karen — invoicing

Reads Priced and Completed job cards, stamps the PESTEL invoice number. Her stamp is the wall — and the last queue in the no-work-vanishes chain.
