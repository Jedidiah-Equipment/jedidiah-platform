# Report a Breakdown

A Breakdown is one problem on one Machine or Implement. Reporting one needs a connection; without signal, [keep a Field Note](./keep-a-field-note) with the photos and report the Breakdown once you are back online.

1. Start from where the problem is:
   - On a Job, choose **Report a problem** on the Machine's card. When it tows an Implement, pick the **Machine** or the **Implement** at the top of the report. The Job is attached for you.
   - On a Machine, choose **Report a problem**.
   - Anywhere else, open **Workshop** and choose **+** (Report a problem), then pick **Machine** or **Implement** and choose it from the list.

   On the web, [report it from Workshop or the Job page](./run-the-workshop-queue#report-a-problem-on-the-web) instead.
2. Check **Already reported**. If the problem is listed, it has been reported: do not report it again. When the report is yours or on your Job, open it and [add a note](./catch-up-on-activity#add-a-note-to-a-job-or-breakdown) instead.
3. Choose **Code Red — machine down** when it cannot work, or **Code Green — still working** when it can carry on for now.
4. Under **What's wrong**, describe the problem in your own words. You can [say it as a Voice Note](./record-a-voice-note) instead of typing.
5. Choose **Take photo** or **Choose from gallery** to add up to six photos. Photos you take here are also saved to the **Jedidiah** album in your gallery.
6. Choose **Send report**. The phone attaches your location when it can.

Once it is sent, you go back to where you started — the Job, the Machine or **Workshop** — and the workshop manager sees it in the Workshop queue. Follow it from **Workshop** or **Activity**: questions and answers go on the Breakdown's **Activity** — choose **Add Note**, typed or as a Voice Note, and **Send**. You can change the urgency, the description and add photos until the Breakdown is Fixed. To change the urgency on the phone, tap the red or green flag beside the Machine or Implement at the top of the Breakdown and choose **Change to Code Green** or **Change to Code Red**. Changing it to Code Red alerts the workshop manager and management as a Code Red does; see [Who gets alerts](./who-gets-workshop-alerts).

Foremen see only the Breakdowns they reported and those on their own Jobs. The workshop manager sees them all.

## Run a Breakdown in the workshop

1. Open the Breakdown from **Workshop**. **Also open on this Job** lists the other Breakdowns on the same Job that are not yet Fixed.
2. Under **Mechanic**, choose the Mechanic who will fix it.
3. Choose **Start work** when the Mechanic starts. The Breakdown is Fixing, and **Started** shows when.
4. Choose **Mark completed**, write the **Close-out note** — what was wrong and what was done — and choose **Confirm completed**. The Breakdown is then Fixed. A Breakdown can go straight to Fixed from Not fixed.

A Fixed Breakdown shows its Mechanic, when work started and was fixed, who closed it out, and the close-out note; only notes can still be added.
