# Sign off a Job

1. Open the Job from **Looks finished**.
2. Check the **Arrival** and **Departure** rows on each Machine card. Use the eye or warning icon beside a reading to open its recorded hours, meter photo, and separate AI assessment. Use the reload icon in the AI section to re-verify a photo, or **Amend reading** to correct the value with a reason.
3. Use the warning icon beside **Arrival** to **Resolve** each Gap Flag. Split the Hour Gap into billable Travel Hours and an Unaccounted Interval, then give a reason.
4. If a Machine is still on site and you have its hours, use the stop icon beside **Departure** to capture its departure reading. Attach a meter photo when available; otherwise give a reason for the reading without a photo.
5. Use the plus icon beside **Measures** to add a measure for each Machine Assignment that Pricing bills per unit, such as hectares or loads. When a measure is recorded, use the edit icon to change or remove it.
6. Enter **Diesel supplied** and **Add charge line** for transport, parts, or a fixed quote. Amounts are set at Pricing.
7. Check the suggested **Start** and **End** dates, then choose **Complete**. Planned Machines that never arrived are removed, and the Job moves to Awaiting pricing.

After Completion, you can still edit the details and amend readings. Once the Job is Priced, its diesel litres and travel no longer change.
