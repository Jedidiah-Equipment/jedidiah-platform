# Capture a reading

1. Open the Machine and choose **Capture reading**, or start or stop a Machine from its Job.
2. Choose **Now** if you are reading the meter now, or **Earlier…** and set when it was read. A photo chosen from the gallery sets the time it was taken. Read At cannot be in the future, and a reading dated earlier must fit between the readings taken before and after it.
3. Under **Meter photo**, choose **Take photo**, allow camera access if asked, keep all digits sharp inside the guide, and choose **Capture**; the guide does not crop the photo. Or choose **Gallery** for a photo taken earlier. Once attached, the photo can be retaken, replaced or removed.
4. Type the **Current reading**, including the decimal digit. **Previous reading** is the Machine's latest reading, the lowest value allowed now. Without a photo the reading carries **Missing Photo Evidence**: say why in the comment.
5. On an arrival, check the **Implement & driver** and choose **Change** if they differ from the plan.
6. If the value is below the previous reading, correct it, choose **Read earlier…** and set when the meter was read, or choose **The previous reading is wrong** to dispute that reading. A John Deere reading cannot be disputed: below one, only the time can fix it. An equal value is accepted.
7. Add a **Comment** if management should know something about this reading, such as a damaged meter or a replaced display. A departure asks for a **Reason** instead, and requires one when management captures it without a photo. You can [say it as a Voice Note](./record-a-voice-note) instead of typing it.
8. Choose **Save reading**. The reading is saved on the server at once; if it is refused, the reason is shown and you can correct the value or its Read At, or dispute the previous reading. When John Deere's readings put the value at an earlier time, **Earlier…** is filled in with that time and the reports it came from: check it, change it if you know better, and choose **Save reading** again. Nothing is saved until you do.

Capturing needs a connection. Without one, [keep a Field Note](./keep-a-field-note) and capture the reading when you are back online. Management reviews photo verification on web; you do not need to wait for it.

## On the web

Management can capture a spot reading from the office, from a photo of the meter someone sends in.

1. Open **Fleet → Machines** and open the Machine.
2. Choose **Capture hours**. Choose **Now**, or **Earlier…** and set **Read At** to when the photo was taken. It cannot be in the future.
3. Type the **Current reading**; **Previous reading** is the lowest value allowed now, and a lower value offers **Read earlier…** and the same dispute as on the phone.
4. Under **Meter photo**, choose the photo. Without one the reading carries **Missing Photo Evidence**.
5. Add a **Comment** if needed and choose **Capture**. A time John Deere proposes for a refused value fills in **Earlier…** as on the phone; check it and choose **Capture** again.
