# Resolve Reading Exceptions

1. Open **Reading exceptions** while signed in as a Contracting Manager or Contracting Administrator.
2. Check the Machine, Read At, Reading type, Hours, Capture comment, Evidence, and AI meter result. **Disputed** and **AI flagged** badges explain why each row needs attention; a row can show both. Choose the **Photo-backed** evidence to preview the captured photo. **Missing Photo Evidence** means no photo was captured; **AI-verified** means the server's reading agrees with the typed value. Hover **No readable meter detected** to see the AI confidence.
3. Choose **Resolve** on a row to open the reading: its recorded hours, its Read At and who captured it, the meter photo, and the AI assessment. For a disputed pair, inspect both readings, then choose **Amend reading** on the incorrect one, enter the **Corrected reading** and, if the meter was read at another time, the **Corrected Read At**, provide an **Amendment reason**, and choose **Amend reading**. The corrected reading is judged again at its Read At: its hours must fit between its neighbouring readings, cannot rise faster than the clock, and its stint stays in time order. Resolving the pair clears its dispute flags and records who amended it, when, and why. A reading that [John Deere](./john-deere-readings) contradicted has no pair: its row says what John Deere reported, and correcting its hours or Read At so it sits in sequence with that report clears the flag. A reading captured days late with Read At left at the time of capture usually needs only its Read At corrected. When a stint's arrival and departure both need an earlier Read At, correct the arrival first.
4. If the typed value is correct and the AI warning is wrong, choose **Resolve**, then **Amend reading**, keep the **Corrected reading** and **Corrected Read At** unchanged, and explain your photo review in the **Amendment reason**. **Amend reading** acknowledges the evidence warning without changing the original AI result or labelling it AI-verified.
5. For an uncertain photo result, choose **Re-verify**. A reading still waiting for its photo result is not listed here; it shows as a purple notice on the Job's Machine card, where its reading also offers **Re-verify photo**. Check the refreshed confidence and outcome. Re-verification reopens any evidence warning for a fresh review. Confidence below 80% remains a low-confidence exception. A possible tenths-drum misread is highlighted when the values differ by about ten times.
6. Repeat until the remaining exceptions have been investigated. If re-verification fails, the previous evidence is kept and an error asks you to try again. Reviewed evidence warnings leave the queue.
