# Resolve Reading Exceptions

1. Open **Reading exceptions** while signed in as a Contracting Manager or Contracting Administrator.
2. Check the Machine, Capture, Reading type, Hours, Capture comment, Evidence, and AI meter result. **Disputed** and **AI flagged** badges explain why each row needs attention; a row can show both. Choose the **Photo-backed** evidence to preview the captured photo. **Missing Photo Evidence** means no photo was captured; **AI-verified** means the server's reading agrees with the typed value. Hover **No readable meter detected** to see the AI confidence.
3. For a disputed pair, inspect both readings. Choose **Resolve** on the incorrect reading, enter the correct **Hours**, and provide an **Amendment reason**. Choose **Amend reading**. The corrected value must fit between its neighbouring readings; resolving the pair clears its dispute flags and records who amended it, when, and why.
4. If the typed value is correct and the AI warning is wrong, choose **Resolve**, keep the correct **Hours**, and explain your photo review in the **Amendment reason**. **Amend reading** acknowledges the evidence warning without changing the original AI result or labelling it AI-verified.
5. For a pending or uncertain photo result, choose **Re-verify**. Check the refreshed confidence and outcome. Re-verification reopens any evidence warning for a fresh review. Confidence below 80% remains a low-confidence exception. A possible tenths-drum misread is highlighted when the values differ by about ten times.
6. Repeat until the remaining exceptions have been investigated. If re-verification fails, the previous evidence is kept and an error asks you to try again. Reviewed evidence warnings leave the queue.
