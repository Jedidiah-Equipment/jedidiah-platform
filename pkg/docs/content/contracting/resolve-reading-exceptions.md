# Resolve Reading Exceptions

1. Open **Reading exceptions** while signed in as a Contracting Manager or Contracting Admin.
2. Check the Machine, captured hours, AI reading, and confidence. Choose **View photo** where evidence is available. **Missing Photo Evidence** means no photo was captured; **AI-verified** means the AI reading agrees to one decimal place.
3. For a disputed pair, inspect both readings. Choose **Amend** on the incorrect reading, enter the correct **Hours**, and provide an **Amendment reason**. Choose **Amend reading**. The corrected value must fit between its neighbouring readings; resolving the pair clears its dispute flags and records who amended it, when, and why.
4. If the typed value is correct and the AI warning is wrong, choose **Amend**, keep the correct **Hours**, and explain your photo review in the **Amendment reason**. **Amend reading** acknowledges the evidence warning without changing the original AI result or labelling it AI-verified.
5. For a pending or uncertain photo result, choose **Re-verify**. Check the refreshed confidence and outcome. Re-verification reopens any evidence warning for a fresh review. Confidence below 80% remains a low-confidence exception. A possible tenths-drum misread is highlighted when the values differ by about ten times.
6. Repeat until the remaining exceptions have been investigated. A failed AI attempt stays pending, while reviewed evidence warnings leave the queue.
