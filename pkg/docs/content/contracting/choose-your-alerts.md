# Choose your alerts

The phone app's own **Settings** page, the same in Contracting and Equipment, shows what the app may use on your phone and which alerts you get.

1. Open the menu and choose **Settings**.
2. Under **Permissions**, each thing the app uses reads **Allowed**, **Not asked yet** or **Not allowed**.
   - Tap **Allow** and answer the phone's question.
   - If the button reads **Open Settings**, tap it, turn the permission on in the phone's settings, and come back to the app. The row updates by itself.
3. Under **Notifications**, turn off any alert you no longer want. It stops that alert's push notifications to you on every phone you sign in to; the Workshop queue, **Activity** and the Dashboard stay the same. Turn it on again to get it back.

If your role gets no alerts, **Notifications** says so; [Who gets alerts](./who-gets-workshop-alerts) lists which roles get which alert.
