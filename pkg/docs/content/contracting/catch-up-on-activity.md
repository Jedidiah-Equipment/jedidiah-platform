# Catch up on Activity

Activity on the phone is one feed, newest first, of what has happened and what has been said on the Jobs and Breakdowns you can see: a Foreman sees their own Jobs and their own Breakdowns, the workshop and management see them all.

1. Open **Activity**. Entries are grouped by day.
2. To narrow the feed, use the filter:
   - **All** shows everything.
   - **Notes** shows Job Notes and Breakdown Notes.
   - **Breakdowns** shows a Breakdown reported, a Mechanic assigned, work started, and a Breakdown Fixed.
   - **Jobs** shows a Job started (its first Machine arrived), signed off, or cancelled.
   - **Hours** shows a Machine arriving on site and leaving, each with its reading.
3. To find something, type in the search box: note text, a person's name, a Job Number, a farm, a customer, a Machine or Implement code, a Mechanic, a close-out note, or a cancellation reason.
4. Pull down to refresh. Scroll down to load older entries.
5. Choose an entry to open its Breakdown, or its Job when you work Jobs on the phone.

The orange dot on **Activity** means something new arrived since you last read it. It clears when you open **Activity** on **All** with nothing in the search box.

## Add a note to a Job or Breakdown

1. Open the Job or the Breakdown, from **Activity**, **Jobs** or **Workshop**.
2. Switch from **Details** to **Activity**. It lists everything on that Job, its Breakdowns included, or everything on that Breakdown.
3. Choose **Add Note**, type the note or [say it as a Voice Note](./record-a-voice-note), and choose **Send**.

The note appears in the thread and in **Activity**. A Foreman can add a Job Note on their own Job until it is Priced; management can add one on a Job in any status. The workshop reads every Job's notes in **Activity** and adds its own on Breakdowns.

## Catch up on the web

1. Open **Activity**, directly under **Dashboard**. An orange dot beside it means something new arrived since you last read it.
2. Choose **All**, **Notes**, **Breakdowns**, **Jobs** or **Hours** to narrow the feed, and type in the search box to find something, as on the phone. Scroll down to load older entries.
3. Choose an entry to open its Breakdown, or its Job when you work Jobs on the web.

The dot clears when you open **Activity** on **All** with nothing in the search box.

To add a note on the web, open the Job or the Breakdown, choose **Add Note** on its **Activity** card, write the note, and choose **Send**. A Job's card lists everything on that Job, its Breakdowns included; a Breakdown's card lists only that Breakdown.

Field Notes are not Activity: they stay on the phone in their own **Field Notes** tab — see [Keep a Field Note](./keep-a-field-note).
