# Run the Workshop queue

The Workshop queue is every Breakdown reported from the field, for the workshop manager to work through on the web.

1. Open **Workshop**.
2. Read the queue. It shows Open and In Progress Breakdowns, newest first; sort the **Urgency** column to bring Code Red to the top. Choose **Open**, **In Progress** or **Solved** to see only those, and choose it again to go back.
3. Check **Dispatch**. **+N on this Job** means other fleet on the same Job also has an open Breakdown: one trip can fix several.
4. Open a Breakdown. Read the **Report** and **Photos**; choose **Open in Google Maps** to see where it was reported. **Also open on this Job** lists the other unsolved Breakdowns on the same Job, so one Mechanic can take them all.
5. Under **Mechanic**, choose the Mechanic who will fix it. You can also assign one straight from the queue.
6. Choose **Start work** when the Mechanic starts. The Breakdown is In Progress.
7. Keep the conversation with the Foreman under **Notes**: write it and choose **Add note**.
8. Choose **Mark solved**, write the **Close-out note** — what was wrong and what was done — and choose **Mark solved**. A Breakdown can be marked Solved straight from Open. Once Solved, only notes can be added.

Foremen follow their own Breakdowns on the phone: see [Report a Breakdown](./report-a-breakdown).
