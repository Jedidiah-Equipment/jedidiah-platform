# Resolve Reading Exceptions

1. Open **Reading exceptions** while signed in as a Contracting Manager or Contracting Administrator.
2. Each row is either a **Dispute** or one **AI flagged** reading. A Dispute is a group of Disputed readings on one Machine: a reading a Foreman said was below a wrong previous reading together with that previous reading, and any other Disputed reading on the same stint. Check the **Machine**, the **Job(s)** each reading belongs to, the **Stint window** (when the Machine arrived and left, or **open** while it is still on site), the **Hours** of each reading, and **Why** the row needs a look: what [John Deere](./john-deere-readings) reported, that the previous reading is wrong, or what the AI read from the photo.
3. Choose **Resolve** on a Dispute to open every reading in it at once. Each card shows the reading's role, its Job and stint window, its **Recorded reading** and **Recorded Read At**, the capture comment, and, only when a photo was captured, the meter photo and the AI assessment. When John Deere contradicted the readings, what it reported is shown first; that report is never edited. Correct the **Corrected reading** or **Corrected Read At** of any reading until they all sit in sequence, enter one **Resolution reason**, and choose **Resolve**. The readings are judged together at their new times: hours must fit between the neighbouring readings, cannot rise faster than the clock, and the Machine's stints stay in time order, so a stint's arrival and departure can move together. If they still do not fit, the reason shows in the dialog and the reading that broke the rule is outlined. A reading captured days late with Read At left at the time of capture usually needs only its Read At corrected. Resolving records who amended each reading, when, and why, and the row leaves the list once every reading sits in sequence.
4. If the typed value is correct and the AI warning is wrong, choose **Resolve** on the **AI flagged** row, then **Amend reading**, keep the **Corrected reading** and **Corrected Read At** unchanged, and explain your photo review in the **Amendment reason**. **Amend reading** acknowledges the evidence warning without changing the original AI result or labelling it AI-verified.
5. For an uncertain photo result, choose **Resolve**, then **Re-verify photo**. A reading still waiting for its photo result is not listed here; it shows as a purple notice on the Job's Machine card, where its reading also offers **Re-verify photo**. Check the refreshed confidence and outcome. Re-verification reopens any evidence warning for a fresh review. Confidence below 80% remains a low-confidence exception. A possible tenths-drum misread is highlighted when the values differ by about ten times.
6. Repeat until the remaining exceptions have been investigated. If re-verification fails, the previous evidence is kept and an error asks you to try again. Reviewed evidence warnings leave the queue.
