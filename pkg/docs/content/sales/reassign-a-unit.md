# Reassign a Unit

Administrators and Super Administrators can move a Product Unit — and the Job building it — from wherever it sits to a different accepted Quote. Use this when one Customer needs a machine sooner than their own build will be ready, when two deals should swap machines, or when a Quote with a Job has to be [re-quoted at new prices](/sales/change-an-accepted-quote#re-quote-a-quote-that-has-a-job).

1. Open the Quote that should receive the machine.
2. Click **Reassign Unit…**.
3. Search for and select the Unit, then click **Continue**.
4. Read what the move will do, add a reason if it helps, then click **Reassign Unit**.

Both deals must be un-invoiced: the Quote receiving the machine, and the Quote that sold the one being moved. Completion is not a barrier — a finished machine standing in the yard is exactly the one a Customer wants sooner.

## What happens to the other deal

If the receiving Quote is already building a machine, that machine returns to Stock and its Job continues as a Stock Build. The deal the moved machine came from stays accepted and locked with no live Job, and its Quote page says so. Nothing is created automatically: start a new Job there, reassign a second machine onto it — a swap is this procedure run twice — or cancel it once a new Quote has replaced it.

## What the specification difference means

The confirm step lists Optional Assemblies fitted to the incoming machine that this Quote did not sell, and the ones this Quote sold that the machine does not carry. It never blocks the move. Pricing and specification stay exactly as the Quote agreed them. The receiving Quote locks once the Job arrives, so amend it before you reassign if the deal has really changed.

## Where the trail shows up

The machine's Unit page records an Ownership Transfer from its previous Owner straight to the receiving Customer, carrying the receiving Quote and your reason. When the machine stays with the same Customer, no Transfer is recorded: the history keeps naming the Quote that first sold it. A displaced machine gets its own Transfer back to Stock. Both Jobs record the change of Quote in their audit history.
