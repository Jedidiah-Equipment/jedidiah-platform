# Capture a reading

1. Open the Machine and choose **Capture reading**, or start or stop a Machine from its Job.
2. Under **Meter photo**, choose **Take photo**, allow camera access if asked, keep all digits sharp inside the guide, and choose **Capture**; the guide does not crop the photo. Or choose **Gallery** for a photo taken earlier. Once attached, the photo can be retaken, replaced or removed.
3. Type the **Current reading**, including the decimal digit. **Previous reading** is the Machine's latest reading, the lowest value allowed. Without a photo the reading carries **Missing Photo Evidence**: say why in the comment.
4. On an arrival, check the **Implement & driver** and choose **Change** if they differ from the plan. Then check **Read At**. It is now, or the time a chosen photo was taken; change it if the meter was read at another time. It cannot be in the future.
5. If the value is below the previous reading, correct it or choose **The previous reading is wrong** to dispute that reading. An equal value is accepted.
6. Add a **Comment** if management should know something about this reading, such as a damaged meter or a replaced display. A departure asks for a **Reason** instead, and requires one when management captures it without a photo.
7. Choose **Save reading**. The reading is saved on the server at once; if it is refused, the reason is shown and you can correct the value or dispute the previous reading.

**Previous reading** for a departure is the arrival you captured.

Capturing needs a connection. Without one, [keep a Field Note](./keep-a-field-note) and capture the reading when you are back online. Management reviews photo verification on web; you do not need to wait for it.
