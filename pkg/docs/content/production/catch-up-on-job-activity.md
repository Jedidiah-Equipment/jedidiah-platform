# Catch up on Job Activity

Use **Jobs → Activity** to read the General Feedback people have submitted across every Job, newest
first, without opening each Job in turn.

1. Choose **Jobs**, then **Activity**.
2. Read down the feed. Each entry names its submitter, when it was submitted, the Job it is about, and
   the feedback itself.
3. Choose **Show more** on a long entry to read the rest of it.
4. Choose an entry to open that Job, where you can read its full feedback and submit your own with
   **Give feedback**.
5. Choose **Open on the Gantt** on an entry to open that Job in Planning instead.
6. Choose **Load more** to fetch the next batch of entries.

The feed shows General Feedback about Jobs only. Corrective Feedback about a department or a person
stays in the Feedback inbox, and feedback submitted about a Quote is not shown here.

Every entry stays in the feed — it is a record of what was said and when, not a queue to work through.
