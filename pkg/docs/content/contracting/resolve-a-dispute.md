# Resolve a Dispute

1. Open **Disputes** while signed in as a Contracting Manager or Contracting Administrator.
2. Each row is a **Dispute**, the Disputed readings on one Machine that are resolved together. Check the **Machine**, the **Job(s)** each reading belongs to, the **Stint window** (when the Machine arrived and left, or **open** while it is still on site), the **Hours** of each reading, and **Why** the row needs a look: what [John Deere](./john-deere-readings) reported, or that the previous reading is wrong.
3. Choose **Resolve** to open every reading in it at once. Each card shows the reading's role, its Job and stint window, its **Recorded reading** and **Recorded Read At**, and the capture comment. When John Deere contradicted the readings, what it reported is shown first; that report is never edited. Correct the **Corrected reading** or **Corrected Read At** of any reading until they all sit in sequence, enter one **Resolution reason**, and choose **Resolve**. The readings are judged together at their new times: hours must fit between the neighbouring readings, cannot rise faster than the clock, and the Machine's stints stay in time order, so a stint's arrival and departure can move together. If they still do not fit, the dialog says which reading breaks which rule. A reading captured days late with Read At left at the time of capture usually needs only its Read At corrected. Resolving records who amended each reading, when, and why, and the row leaves the list once every reading sits in sequence.
