# Set up a Job

1. Open **Jobs**. The list shows every Job still in progress, with its **Stage**. Choose a stage button such as Upcoming, Looks finished or Invoiced to show only that stage, and choose it again to show them all. To find Cancelled Jobs, filter the **Stage** column.
2. Choose **New job**. Select the **Customer**, **Farm**, and **Work type**, assign a **Foreman**, and add a description. If the Customer, Farm, or Work type is new, type its name and choose **Create** to add it without leaving the form. You can assign a Foreman later from Upcoming. Save the Job. On the phone, **Jobs → New job** offers the same form; plan Machines from the Job screen afterwards.
3. Under **Machines**, choose **Plan machine** for each Machine expected on site. Select its Implement and Driver. The Foreman can also add an unplanned Machine while working the Job.
4. When a planned Machine arrives, choose **Start — capture arrival**. If its Implement or Driver changed, use the **Edit implement and driver** pencil button, select the actual ones, and choose **Apply**. Enter its arrival hours and choose **Save hours** to capture the arrival and save its starting Assignment. See [Capture Job hours](./capture-job-hours). The Job becomes **Active** when the first Machine arrives. It appears in **Looks finished** after every Machine has left.
5. To take off a Machine added by mistake, choose **Remove** on its card and confirm. Once it has arrived, give a reason: its hours, Travel and Measures are deleted, and the Machine's other stints on the Job stay. Management can do this until the Job is Invoiced; on a Priced Job it returns the Job to pricing. If it was the only Machine that arrived on an Active Job, the Job goes back to Upcoming.

When the work is done, [sign off the Job](./sign-off-a-job.md).
