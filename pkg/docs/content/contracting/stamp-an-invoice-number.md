# Stamp an Invoice Number

1. Open **Invoicing**. It shows **Awaiting invoice** first: every Priced Job with its **Total ex VAT**. When nothing is waiting, it shows every Job instead.
2. Open the Job to read its Job Card figures — Machines, hours, Rates, Charge Lines, Diesel (VAT-exempt) and Discount — and key them into the accounting system, or choose **Job card** to preview and download them.
3. Back in the list, choose **Stamp**, enter the **Invoice number** exactly as the accounting system printed it, and choose **Stamp**. If another Job already carries that number it is shown; one invoice may cover more than one Job.
4. The Job moves to **Invoiced** and nothing on it can change again. To reconcile against the accounting system, choose **All**: it lists awaiting and invoiced Jobs, newest invoice first. The filter on the **Invoiced** column narrows it to invoices stamped in a date range, and the **Stage** filter picks one stage.

If a Job's total looks wrong, do not stamp it: ask Pricing to review it. A Priced Job that is re-priced leaves Awaiting invoice until it is marked Priced again.
