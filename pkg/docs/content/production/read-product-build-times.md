# Read Product build times

## Compare one Department's build times

1. Open the Product and choose **Build times**.
2. Choose **Fabrication**, **Paint**, **Assembly**, or **Workshop**.
3. Read the average working days and build count. A Product with no completed stamps for that
   Department shows **—** instead.
4. Under **Builds counted**, compare each Job's scheduled and actual days, crew size, and done date.
   Choose a Job Number to open that Job.

Only Build Jobs with both Department stamps are counted. Figures begin when a Department is stamped
done; older unstamped builds are not filled in later.

Administrators also see **Fabrication crew**, **Paint crew**, **Assembly crew**, or **Workshop crew**
with each Crew member's average days, build count, and average crew size.
