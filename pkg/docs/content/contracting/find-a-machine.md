# Find a Machine

1. Open **Jedidiah Contracting** on your phone, then open the **Machines** tab.
2. Search by Machine Code, make, or model. Open the filter menu and choose a Category to narrow the list, or **All categories** to clear it.
3. Use the sort menu to order by **Machine Code** or **Category**. Your filter and sort choices are remembered.
4. **On Job** shows the Job a Machine is currently on; otherwise it shows **In Yard**. Open the Machine to read its hours.

Open Machines while connected before going into the field. The saved fleet remains available offline on this phone for your signed-in account.
