# Send Feedback on a Purchase Order

1. Open a Purchase Order and choose **Send Feedback** in its **Feedback** card.
   From a linked Job instead, choose **Send PO Feedback** in its Activity and select the **Purchase Order**.
   On mobile, use the Job’s **Activity** tab and **Send PO Feedback**.
2. Enter your **Feedback**.
3. Choose **Submit Feedback**.

You can submit in any Purchase Order status, including Cancelled. Your name, date and time are recorded
automatically. Feedback is permanent: nobody can edit or delete it, including administrators. To correct
something, submit new Feedback.

Everyone who can read the order or a currently linked Job can see it, newest first. The same Feedback
appears on every currently linked Job, including when a Job is linked after submission. It leaves a
Job’s Activity if that link is removed. A restock order’s Feedback stays on the order.
