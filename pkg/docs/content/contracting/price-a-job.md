# Price a Job

1. Open **Jobs**, go to **Awaiting pricing**, and choose **Price** on the Job.
2. For each Machine Assignment, pick a **Rate**. A time Rate bills the work hours plus included travel; a measure Rate bills that Assignment's Measure. Pick **No charge** when a Charge Line carries a fixed quote. Picking a Rate on a Machine's first Assignment fills its other Assignments that have no Rate yet.
3. If a measure Rate bills nothing because no Measure is recorded, choose **Add measure** on that Assignment.
4. To change a computed amount, click it and type the new amount. **Reset to computed** undoes the edit, and choosing another Rate discards it.
5. Enter the **Diesel** price per litre. Diesel is VAT-exempt, and its amount can be edited like any other. In **Charge lines**, enter an amount on every line; zero is allowed.
6. Optionally set a **Discount** in rands (**R**) or as a percentage (**%**). It applies to Machine Assignments and Charge Lines, never to Diesel.
7. Check the **Total ex VAT** and choose **Mark as Priced**. The amounts freeze and the Job moves to **Awaiting invoice**.

Amending an Hour Reading on a Priced Job returns it here with its amounts recomputed and any edited amounts reset; an Invoiced Job cannot change.
