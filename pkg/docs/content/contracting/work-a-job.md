# Work a Job

1. Open **Jobs**. Your open Jobs are listed, newest first. Search by Job, customer, or farm; use the filter to pick one stage, such as **Awaiting pricing**; and sort by **Newest** or **Name**.
2. Open the Job. Each Machine shows **Planned**, **On site**, or **Left site**.
3. To start a planned Machine, choose **Start — capture arrival** and [capture its hours](./capture-job-hours). Change the Implement or Driver there if they differ from the plan.
4. To stop it, choose **Stop — capture departure**. A Machine that has left shows its arrival and departure hours, its Work hours and its Travel hours.
5. To correct your hours while the Job is open, choose **Edit hours**. A Machine that needs a look shows why in amber.
6. To record what a Machine produced, such as hectares disked or loads hauled, choose **Add measure** on it once it has arrived, pick the Measure Type, enter the quantity, and choose **Save measure**. Recorded Measures show on the Machine; choose **Edit measures** to change one by picking its Measure Type again, or to remove one. A Foreman can do this while the Job is Active.
7. To add a Machine that was not planned, choose **Add machine**, pick the Machine, attach an Implement if it tows one, confirm the Driver, and confirm with **Add machine**. It joins the list as **Planned**; start it as in step 3 when it arrives. A Machine still on another Job can be planned but cannot start until it leaves there. A Machine that left can be added again the same way.
8. To read or add to the Job's notes, switch from **Details** to **Activity**: choose **Add Note**, type it or say it as a Voice Note, and choose **Send**. A Foreman can add one until the Job is Priced. See [Catch up on Activity](./catch-up-on-activity).

The Jobs screen needs a connection; without one, [keep a Field Note](./keep-a-field-note).
