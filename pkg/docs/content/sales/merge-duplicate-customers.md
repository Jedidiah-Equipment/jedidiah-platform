# Merge duplicate Customers

Procurement Managers, Administrators, and Super Administrators can merge a duplicate Customer into the Customer that should remain.

1. Open the duplicate Customer that should be removed.
2. Click **Merge into…**.
3. Search for and select the Customer that should remain, then click **Continue**. Use the contact person and creation date to distinguish Customers with the same name.
4. Review the Quote and Unit counts, then click **Merge customer**. The Unit count shows Units the duplicate currently owns.

Every Quote and every Unit's ownership history move to the remaining Customer, whose empty contact fields are filled from the duplicate while existing values stay unchanged; the duplicate is deleted permanently with no undo, and generated Quote Documents keep the name they were printed with.
