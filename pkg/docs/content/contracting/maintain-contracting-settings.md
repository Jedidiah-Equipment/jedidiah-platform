# Maintain Contracting Settings

Settings is the one row of Contracting-wide values every screen reads live; nothing already stored is re-judged.

1. In Jedidiah Contracting, open **Settings** under Admin.
2. Change **Travel Flag threshold (hours)**. Travel Hours above this mark the Machine on a Job as needing a look; nothing is blocked.
3. Change **John Deere difference threshold (hours)**. Job hours this far from John Deere's last reading warn the person capturing and mark the Machine as needing a look.
4. Change **Service Due Soon threshold (hours)**. A Machine is Service Due Soon within this many hours of its Next Service Due, unless it sets its own **Service alert hours**. Alerts fire at the next capture that brings a Machine inside the threshold.
5. Change **Utilisation Target (%)**. Drawn as the reference line on the Utilisation report. Leave empty to draw no line.
6. Choose **Save**. Nothing changes until you do, and each save is one entry in the Audit Log under Settings.

Only a **Contracting Administrator** or a **Super Administrator** can open this page or save it.
