# Stamp an Invoice Number

1. Open **Invoicing**. **Awaiting invoice** lists every Priced Job with its **Total ex VAT**.
2. Open the Job to read its Job Card figures — Machines, hours, Rates, Charge Lines, Diesel (VAT-exempt) and Discount — and key them into the accounting system, or choose **Job card** to print the figures.
3. Back in the queue, choose **Stamp**, enter the **Invoice number** exactly as the accounting system printed it, and choose **Stamp**. If another Job already carries that number it is shown; one invoice may cover more than one Job.
4. The Job moves to **Invoiced** and nothing on it can change again. Use the **Invoiced** tab and its month filter to reconcile against the accounting system.

If a Job's total looks wrong, do not stamp it: ask Pricing to review it. A Priced Job that is re-priced leaves this queue until it is marked Priced again.
