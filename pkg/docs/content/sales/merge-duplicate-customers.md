# Merge duplicate Customers

Procurement Managers, Administrators, and Super Administrators can merge a duplicate Customer into the Customer that should remain.

1. Open the duplicate Customer that should be removed.
2. Click **Merge into…**.
3. Search for and select the Customer that should remain, then click **Continue**. Use the contact person and creation date to distinguish Customers with the same name.
4. Review the Quote and Unit counts, then click **Merge customer**.

Every Quote and the ownership history of every Unit move to the remaining Customer. The Unit count shows Units the duplicate currently owns. Empty contact fields on the remaining Customer are filled from the duplicate while existing values stay unchanged, and the duplicate is deleted permanently with no undo. Quote Documents already generated keep the name they were printed with.
