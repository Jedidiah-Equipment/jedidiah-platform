# Connect John Deere

The John Deere Connection is Contracting's one link to the owner's John Deere Operations Center organization. Connect it with the owner's John Deere login, because Jedidiah can then read exactly what the owner can.

## Connect

1. In Jedidiah Contracting, open **Settings** under Admin and choose the **John Deere** tab.
2. Choose **Connect**. The browser goes to John Deere.
3. Sign in with the owner's John Deere login and accept what Jedidiah asks to read.
4. If John Deere asks which organization Jedidiah may use, choose the owner's organization and confirm.
5. The browser comes back to the John Deere tab, which reads **Connected** with the organization's name, who connected it and when.

## Reconnect

Choose **Reconnect** when the tab reads **Needs reconnecting**, or to give Jedidiah a different organization. It is the same sign-in as Connect, and the connection keeps working until John Deere answers.

## Disconnect

Choose **Disconnect** and confirm. Jedidiah forgets the John Deere sign-in and stops reading from John Deere until someone connects again. Hours already stored stay.

## What the tab says

- **Connected**: Jedidiah reads the organization named on the tab.
- **Needs reconnecting**: John Deere stopped accepting the sign-in, or no organization gave Jedidiah access. The tab shows why; choose **Reconnect**.
- **Not connected**: no one has connected, or a sign-in was not finished. Choose **Connect**.
- **Not configured**: this environment has no John Deere application keys, so there is nothing to connect yet.

Only a **Contracting Administrator** or a **Super Administrator** can open this tab.
