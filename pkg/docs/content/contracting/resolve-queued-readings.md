# Resolve queued readings

1. When **Needs attention** appears in **Machines**, open it and read the reason beside each capture that could not sync.
2. If another reading landed first, review the last known reading shown (connect to load it if needed). If that value is wrong, choose **The previous reading is wrong · resubmit as dispute**. The original capture and photo return to the queue. If another reading arrives before this dispute syncs, it stays in Needs attention for you to review again.
3. To remove a capture instead, choose **Discard capture**, then **Confirm discard**. This permanently removes the local capture and photo.
4. Keep the app open with a connection, or choose **Try sync now**. Later captures for the same Machine wait until the earlier refused capture is resolved. Other Machines continue syncing.

Captures without a refusal sync automatically while the app is open and connected; they do not show a Needs attention button. Check the Machine's **Reading history** to see queued captures.

A refused start or stop also appears on the Job's Machine card.

A connection or sign-in failure leaves captures queued for retry. If a saved meter photo is no longer available, the capture moves to Needs attention instead of retrying forever; discard it and capture the reading again. If your role no longer permits capture, the refused item also stays in Needs attention. For another refusal that does not offer a dispute, ask management to check the Machine before deciding whether to discard and capture again.
