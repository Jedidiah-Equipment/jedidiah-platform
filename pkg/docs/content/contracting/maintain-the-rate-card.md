# Maintain the Rate Card

The Rate Card is the list of named Rates Pricing chooses from, one per stint. A Rate hangs on nothing but its name — not on a Category, a Machine or an Implement.

1. In Jedidiah Contracting, open **Rate Card**.
2. Choose **New rate**, enter the **Name**, choose the **Basis** — **Time** bills each hour of the stint's work plus its included travel; **Measure** bills each unit of one Measure Type recorded on the stint — pick the **Measure type** when the basis is Measure, enter the **Amount** in rands, and choose **Save**. Names must be unique, ignoring letter case.
3. Drag a Rate by its handle to change the order Pricing's picker shows. The order saves as you drop.
4. Open a Rate to correct its Name, Basis, Measure type or Amount. Changes save automatically; check the save status before leaving.
5. Turn **Active** off to stop offering a Rate in Pricing. Stints already priced with it keep it and their amounts.
6. To remove a Rate no job has used, choose **Delete rate**. A Rate on a priced job cannot be deleted; deactivate it instead.

Changing an Amount never touches a job: a Priced Job holds its own snapshot and a Completed one is priced later from the card as it stands then. Fixed quotes need no zero Rate — Pricing offers **No charge** on every stint.
