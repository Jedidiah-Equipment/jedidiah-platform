# Review mechanic performance

Mechanic performance shows how much Breakdown work each Mechanic solved in a period and how quickly.

1. In Jedidiah Contracting, open **Reports** and choose **Mechanics**.
2. Choose **Month**, **Quarter** or **Year**, then step with **Previous period** and **Next period**.
3. Read each Mechanic's row:
   - **Solved** is the Breakdowns they took to **Fixed** in the period, Machine and Implement alike.
   - **Report-to-Solved** is the average time from a Breakdown being reported to it being solved, in days.
   - **Not solved** is the Breakdowns assigned to them that are **Not fixed** or **Fixing** today; it ignores the period.
4. Read the **Unassigned** row: Breakdowns with no Mechanic, the queue's backlog.
5. Select a Mechanic's name to open the Workshop showing only their Breakdowns, solved ones included.

Every Mechanic appears, and so does anyone who was ever a Breakdown's Mechanic, even after their role changed.
