# Open a Bay plan

Use the mobile app's **Plan** tab to find a Bay and inspect the work in its Bay Queue.

1. Tap **Plan** in the bottom tab bar.
2. Search by Bay, operator, active Job code, Product or work title, or Customer.
3. Open the sort menu and choose **Days left** to surface the most urgent active work, or **Bay name**
   for alphabetical order. Either order applies within a Department, so compare Bays under one heading.
   Idle Bays follow active Bays in the Days left view.
4. Tap a Bay to open its schedule.
5. Tap a Work Slot to see its Job details. On a phone, use **Back** to return from the Slot to the Bay
   schedule, then to Plan.

Plan shows enabled Bays only, grouped under their Department heading in the same fixed pipeline order the
Board uses: Fabrication, Procurement, Supply, Paint, Assembly, Workshop. A Department with no Bay left to
show gets no heading. An idle Bay remains in the list and reads **Idle** until a Work Slot covers today.
