# Send Feedback on a Purchase Order

1. Open a Purchase Order and choose **Send Feedback** in its **Feedback** card.
   From a linked Job instead, choose **Send PO Feedback** in its Activity and select the **Purchase Order**.
   On mobile, use the Job’s **Activity** tab and **Send PO Feedback**.
2. Enter your **Feedback**. Check it before submitting: nobody can edit or delete it, including administrators.
3. Choose **Submit Feedback**. To correct something later, submit new Feedback.

See [Activity](../production/catch-up-on-job-activity) for how Feedback appears on linked Jobs.
