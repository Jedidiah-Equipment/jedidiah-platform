# Review fleet utilisation

Utilisation shows how many days each Machine spent on Jobs, and its Work and Travel hours, for one calendar period.

1. In Jedidiah Contracting, open **Reports**. The **Utilisation** tab opens on the current month.
2. Choose **Month**, **Quarter** or **Year**, then step with **Previous period** and **Next period**. The current period counts only the days up to today.
3. Read the two headline figures. **Fleet Load** is the share of Machines that worked at least one day. **Utilisation** is active days over the days Machines were in the fleet, added up across the whole fleet.
4. Read the chart: twelve months of Utilisation % up to the period you chose. Choose **Show trend** on a Category, or a Machine's row, to chart just that one; choose **Show the fleet** to go back.
5. Read each Machine's hour columns for the period:
   - **Work** is the hours between each arrival and departure, spread evenly over the Job's dates.
   - **Travel** is each stint's Travel Hours, spread the same way.
6. A Machine's active days are the days inside its Jobs' **Start** and **End** dates. A date not set yet comes from the captures: **Start** is the day the first arrival was captured, and **End** the day the last departure was once every Machine has left, or today while one is still on site.

The dashed **Target** line is the Utilisation Target set in [Settings](./maintain-contracting-settings); without one, no line is drawn. Implements are not counted. A Machine's daily hours heat map reads only its spot and John Deere readings, so a Machine with neither shows none.
