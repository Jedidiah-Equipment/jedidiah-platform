# Book a service

A Service Record is the light digital copy of the paper service book: when a Machine was in, its hour reading, who serviced it, and the Next Service Due printed on the dash sticker.

1. Open **Fleet → Machines** and open the Machine.
2. When it comes into the workshop, choose **Book a service**; you can also book one in from [Services](./see-what-is-due-for-a-service). Set the **Start date**, the **Mechanic** if you know it, and any **Notes**, then choose **Book service**. At the Machine, you can book it in from the phone: open it from **Machines** and choose **Book a service** beside **Report a problem**. Closing the service stays on the web.
3. While it is in, change the start date, Mechanic or notes on the service in the workshop; they save automatically.
4. When it leaves, choose **Close** on the service. Enter the **End date**, the **Reading at service**, the **Mechanic** and the **Notes**.
5. Enter the **Next service due** hours you print on the sticker. When the Machine has a **Service interval**, the reading plus the interval is suggested; change it if the sticker says otherwise.
6. Choose **Close service**. The Machine's **Next service due** becomes the number you entered.
7. Check the **Services** card: **Service due soon** means the latest reading is within the Machine's **Service alert hours** of Next service due, or the Service Due Soon threshold in **Settings** (100 hours unless changed) when it has none, **Service overdue** means the meter has passed it, and **Booked in** means a service is open. A spot reading counts too, so [capture one](./capture-a-reading) to keep it current between Jobs.
