# Maintain Customers and Farms

Contracting keeps its own Customer directory, separate from Equipment.

1. In Jedidiah Contracting, open **Customers**.
2. Choose **New customer**, enter the **Name**, and choose **Save**. Names must be unique within Contracting, ignoring letter case.
3. Fill in **Contact name**, **Phone**, **Email**, and **Notes** as needed. Changes save automatically; check the save status before leaving and use **Retry** if saving fails.
4. Under **Farms**, choose **New farm**, enter the **Farm name**, and choose **Save**. The Farm belongs to this Customer. The same name is allowed under another Customer, but cannot repeat here, ignoring letter case.
5. Select a Farm to correct its **Farm name**. Changes save automatically.
6. To remove an unused Farm, select it, choose **Delete farm**, and confirm. A referenced Farm cannot be deleted.
