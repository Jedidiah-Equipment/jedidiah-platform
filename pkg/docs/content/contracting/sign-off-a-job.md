# Sign off a Job

1. Open the Job from **Looks finished**.
2. Check the arrival and departure hours for each Machine. Choose **Edit hours** to correct them; once the Job is signed off a reason is required. See [Capture Job hours](./capture-job-hours).
3. If a Machine is still on site and you have its hours, choose **Stop — capture departure** and capture its hours. The Job cannot be completed while any Machine is still on site.
4. Check each Machine's **Travel**. Machines marked amber need a look: travel over the threshold, travel set to 0, or hours far from John Deere. Choose **Set Travel Hours** to correct travel. None of these stops sign-off.
5. For each Machine Assignment that Pricing bills per unit, such as hectares or loads, choose **+** on it, then **Add measure**. Recorded Measures show as a **Measures** row; choose **Edit measures** on it to change or remove one.
6. Enter **Diesel supplied** and **Add charge line** for transport, parts, or a fixed quote. Amounts are set at Pricing.
7. Check the suggested **Start** and **End** dates, taken from when the first arrival and the last departure were captured, then choose **Complete**. Reports count the Machines' active days from these dates. Planned Machines that never arrived are removed, and the Job moves to Awaiting pricing.

After Completion, you can still edit the details, the hours and the travel. Once the Job is Priced, its diesel litres no longer change; editing hours or travel returns it to pricing.
