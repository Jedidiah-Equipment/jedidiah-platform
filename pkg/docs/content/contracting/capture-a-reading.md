# Capture a reading

1. Open the Machine and choose **Capture reading**.
2. Choose **Photograph meter**, allow camera access if asked, and keep all digits sharp inside the guide. Choose **Take photo**. The guide does not crop the photo.
3. Type the **Hour meter value**, including the decimal digit. **Minimum allowed** is the Machine's latest known reading, including captures still queued on this phone. If the camera is unavailable, continue without a photo: the reading will carry **Missing Photo Evidence**.
4. If the value is below the minimum, correct it or choose **The previous reading is wrong** to dispute that reading. An equal value is accepted.
5. Add a **Comment** if management should know something about this reading, such as a damaged meter or a replaced display.
6. Choose **Save reading to queue**. The Machine page shows the locally saved capture. Keep the app open with a connection to sync; offline captures survive closing and reopening the app.

The same queue is used with or without signal. Photos stay on this phone until their capture syncs. Management reviews photo verification on web; you do not need to wait for it. If another reading lands first and yours is refused, [resolve it in Needs attention](./resolve-queued-readings).
