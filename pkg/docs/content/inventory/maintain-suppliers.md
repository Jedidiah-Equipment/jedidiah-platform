# Maintain Suppliers

A Supplier is the procurement directory record that bought Parts and Purchase Orders belong to.

## Add a Supplier

1. Open **Suppliers**.
2. Click **New supplier**.
3. Enter the company name.
4. Click **Save**. The new Supplier opens so you can add its contact details.

## Update a Supplier

1. Open **Suppliers** and select the Supplier.
2. Update the company or contact details. Changes save automatically.

## Remove a Supplier

1. Open the Supplier and click **Remove supplier**.
2. Confirm the removal.

A Supplier with a Draft Purchase Order cannot be removed because that editable order would be
stranded. Sent and cancelled Purchase Orders keep the Supplier as history.
