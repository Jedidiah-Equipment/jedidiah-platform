# Control public Product Assemblies

**Publicly Visible** controls whether an Assembly appears on the Lander and in the generated Brochure. It does not change Quotes, costing, or production use.

1. Open **Products** and choose the Product.
2. In **Assemblies**, expand the Standard or Optional Assembly.
3. Turn **Publicly visible** on to include the Assembly on both public surfaces, or off to omit it.
4. Wait for the Product to finish saving before leaving the page.

New Assemblies start with **Publicly visible** off.
