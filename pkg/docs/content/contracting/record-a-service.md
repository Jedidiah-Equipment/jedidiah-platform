# Record a service

A Service Record is the light digital copy of the paper service book: when a Machine was in, its hour reading, who serviced it, and the Next Service Due printed on the dash sticker.

1. Open **Fleet → Machines** and open the Machine.
2. When it comes into the workshop, choose **Record a service** under **Service**. Set the **Start date**, the **Mechanic** if you know it, and any **Notes**, then choose **Record service**.
3. While it is in, change the start date, Mechanic or notes on the service in the workshop; they save automatically.
4. When it leaves, choose **Close** on the service. Enter the **End date**, the **Reading at service (hours)**, the **Mechanic** and the **Notes**.
5. Enter the **Next service due (hours)** you print on the sticker. When the Machine has a **Service interval (hours)**, the reading plus the interval is suggested; change it if the sticker says otherwise.
6. Choose **Close service**. The Machine's **Next service due** becomes the number you entered.
7. Check the **Service** card: **Service due soon** means the latest reading is within 100 hours of Next service due, and **Service overdue** means the meter has passed it. A spot reading counts too, so [capture one](./capture-a-reading) to keep it current between Jobs.
